# Phase 0 approval record v03

User approved the proposed 5.8 mm envelope, 18 mm zone pitch, R2 corners, Ø5 domes and PC shell. The user left magnet diameter to engineering judgment: selected Ø57 mm, within the original 56–59 mm brief, leaving 1.5 mm total edge margin per side. A 1.0 mm wall then leaves nominal 0.5 mm radial air clearance at the narrowest X extremes.

Screenshot graphics, no flower, entirely black/white. Flexible clear continuous top and nine tactile zones confirmed. Battery placeholder allowed. Both FDM and resin print checks requested. Apple and Android are target categories; no specific handset/case/charger is selected. User explicitly permits geometric/appearance prototyping with through-charging unverified.

Phase 1 may proceed. No claim of universal phone fit, charging power, magnetic force or electrical functionality. All nine zones and specified components must remain. No silent dimensional reductions to pass checks.

Additional declared implementation assumptions for review in Phase 1:

- Battery moves to upper strip and becomes a 24×10×2.5 mm envelope, not a selected cell; capacity unspecified. This changes the earlier unapproved 24×30 placeholder, not an approved production part.
- BLE reserve 10×12×1.5 and touch IC reserve 6×6×1.0 mm on lower strip; no RF qualification or routing.
- Generic USB-C envelope 8.4×7×2.6 mm; provisional opening 9×3 mm. These are CAD placeholders, not vendor fit dimensions.
- PCB 54×76×0.6 mm with open center and three 6 mm row support rails. Touch flex still covers 54×60 mm; conductors unmodeled. Charging remains unverified despite open regions.
- Continuous clear face 56×64×0.5 mm; 54×60×0.2 mm sensor/bond allocation. Outer finish band 0.1 mm; flush black glyph inlays are separate objects.
- Dome height 0.3 mm, modeled spring-metal skin 0.05 mm; 0.2 mm pressed travel is only a geometric proxy. Actuator diameter 1.5 mm. No force/preload/fatigue claim.
- Four M1.4 unthreaded screw envelopes, Ø2.5×0.5 head, Ø1.6 clearance bores, Ø3.5 boss. No verified retention/insert/thread engagement; screw locations X±24,Y±39 mm.
